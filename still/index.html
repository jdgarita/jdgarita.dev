Why Still

A calmer relationship with the food in your kitchen.

Six ways Still changes how you cook — and what you throw out.

Cook at the peak

Every ingredient has a window when its flavor is at its absolute best. Still tells you when that is — for each item, each storage, each day.

Stay always fresh

Quiet guidance, never alarms. A calm feed that surfaces what to eat, what is resting, and what is defrosting — without the anxiety.

Save the environment

A third of food produced globally is wasted. Tracking freshness on-device prevents the most preventable kind — the kind in your fridge.

Reduce waste

FIFO instance tracking handles duplicates by date so the older batch always gets used first. No more “is this still good?” guessing.

Save money

Catch ingredients at their peak instead of letting them quietly expire in the back of the fridge — and watch your grocery budget go further.

On-device & private

Your kitchen is yours. Still runs entirely on your phone — no cloud, no accounts, no data sold. Your groceries are not a profile.

The Culinary Arc

Three states. No traffic lights.

Other apps shout red when food is “expiring.” Still does the opposite — it tracks the positive arc of flavor, so you know exactly when to cook each item.

Peak
Optimal time to consume — flavor at its absolute best.
Action
Nearing the end of peak. Cook today, freeze, or share.
Resting
Building flavor. Leave alone — it'll be ready soon.

Why it matters

A quieter kitchen, a smaller footprint.

One third of all food is wasted before it's eaten.
Most of that is household waste — the kind that quietly expires in your fridge. It's the easiest waste in the world to prevent, and it's exactly what Still is for.
